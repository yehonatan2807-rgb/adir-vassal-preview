Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 2026

We want this website to be accessible and comfortable for everyone, including people with disabilities. It has been adapted in accordance with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

Conformance level

The site was built to meet Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, level AA.

What we have done

  • A clear heading structure and semantic landmarks for screen-reader users.
  • A “skip to main content” link at the top of every page.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the ring designer, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text alternatives for all informative images.
  • Sufficient colour contrast between text and background.
  • Links that open in a new window are announced to screen readers.
  • The site respects the “reduce motion” system setting: animations are disabled.
  • The site is available in Hebrew and English, works on desktop, tablet and mobile, and supports text zoom up to 200%.
  • Tested on current versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

Limitations

We keep improving the site. Third-party services the site links to (WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps) are outside our control and may not be fully accessible. If you run into any difficulty, please tell us and we will fix it.
