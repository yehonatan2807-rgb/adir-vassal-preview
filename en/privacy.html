Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website, how it is used and what your rights are. It follows Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, and the regulations made under it.

The site is operated by Registered business name, Business / company number (“the business”), Israel Diamond Exchange, Ramat Gan building, floor and office number.

What we collect

There are no forms, sign-ups or user accounts on this site, and the site itself does not collect your name, phone number, email or any other identifying detail.

The ring designer runs entirely in your browser: your choices are not stored or sent to us. Only if you press “Send on WhatsApp” does a pre-written WhatsApp message open – and you decide whether to send it.

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or Instagram, your message goes through that service and we receive only the details you choose to share. We use them solely to answer you, arrange an appointment and provide our service.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses no cookies, analytics, social-media pixels or targeted advertising.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. To do so, your browser sends your IP address and basic technical data to Google, subject to Google's privacy policy.

As with any website, the hosting server may keep technical access logs (IP address, browser type, time of access) for security and operation only.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps lead to external services governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they process information.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or pass on personal information, except where needed to provide a service you asked for (such as shipping or insurance), where required by law, or with your consent.

Marketing

We will not send you marketing messages without your prior explicit consent, in accordance with section 30A of the Communications (Telecommunications and Broadcasting) Law, 1982. You can opt out at any time.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information you give us, and keep it only as long as needed to provide our service or as required by law (for example for tax and warranty purposes).

Your rights

Under the Protection of Privacy Law you may review information held about you and ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date. Contact: +972 50-464-6220 or contact email.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page with its date. The Hebrew version is the official one.
