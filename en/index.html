About

Adir Vassal

The moment that moves me most isn't the sale.

Adir Vassal

I work from inside the Israel Diamond Exchange in Ramat Gan, where diamonds are bought, examined and change hands. That is where I choose the stones I show my clients.

I would rather fit the diamond to the ring than the ring to whatever happens to be in stock. So every meeting starts with a few stones on the table and a plain explanation of how they differ.

From the first meeting until the piece is in your hands, you deal with me.
